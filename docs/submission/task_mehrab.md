## How to record

**Camera**
- Use a phone, back camera, held **sideways (wide)**. Set it to 1080p, 30 frames per second.
- Put the phone on a stand or a stack of books. Do not hold it in your hand.
- The lens is at the height of your eyes, about one arm away from you.

**Where you stand in the picture**
- Stand or sit in the **right half** of the picture. Leave the **left half empty**. Mehedi puts the list of
  data names and a figure there.
- Your head and shoulders fill the right half. Leave a little space above your head.
- Turn your body slightly toward the empty side. Look into the lens, not at the screen.

**The room**
- A plain wall behind you. No window, no mirror, no poster, no mess behind you.
- Light on your face from the front: face a window, or put a lamp behind the phone. No strong light behind you.
- A quiet room. Fan and air conditioner off. Door closed. Phone on flight mode.
- Wear a plain dark top with no text and no logo.

**Sound**
- Use a wired earphone microphone, or put a second phone 15 cm from your mouth, just out of the picture,
  and record your voice on it at the same time.
- Clap your hands once at the start of each recording. This helps Mehedi line up the sound.

**Takes**
- Record each take **three times**.
- Stay still and silent for 2 seconds before you speak and 2 seconds after.
- If you make a mistake, stop, wait 2 seconds, and say the whole sentence again. Do not restart the recording.

## How to say it

- Sure and steady. This is the part where the judges decide if the work is real.
- Read the lines aloud five times before you record, so you are not reading from a paper on camera.
- You may stick the lines next to the phone lens, at eye height.
