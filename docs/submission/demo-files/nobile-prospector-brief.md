# Mission brief: Nobile Prospector

Example brief for a demonstration. Not a real mission.

- Provider: Astrobotic
- Lander: Griffin
- Launch vehicle: Falcon 9 from LC-39A, Kennedy Space Center
- Planned launch: 12 June 2028
- Landing planned for 20 June 2028
- Landing site: Nobile rim 1
- Surface operations: 9 days
- Payloads: a neutron spectrometer (4.2 kg), a drill (18 kg) and a mass spectrometer (7.4 kg)
