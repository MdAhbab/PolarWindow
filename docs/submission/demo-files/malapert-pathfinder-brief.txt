Mission brief: Malapert Pathfinder
Provider: Firefly Aerospace. Lander: Blue Ghost.
Launch vehicle: Falcon 9 from LC-39A, Kennedy Space Center.
Planned launch: 9 October 2028. Landing planned for 24 November 2028.
Landing site: Malapert massif.
Surface operations: 12 days.
Payloads: a radio relay test package (6.5 kg) and a thermal camera (2.1 kg).
